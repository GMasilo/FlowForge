About FlowForge

FlowForge is a business application for building chatbots, automating workflows, connecting services, managing structured data and supporting customer conversations.

What the application does

  • Design conversational flows with questions, branching and reusable steps.
  • Connect business services through APIs and webhooks.
  • Manage structured records and collect information through chat.
  • Review conversation analytics and hand conversations over to support agents.
  • Test and publish chatbots that can be embedded on websites.

Who uses FlowForge

Teams use FlowForge to build customer support, information collection and business process applications. The management application requires an account; published chatbots may be made publicly accessible by their owners.
